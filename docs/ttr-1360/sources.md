# TTR-1360 sources

Every medical statement in the two pieces is taken from these pages. Practice
facts (services, Dr. Adili's training, address, night guard materials) come from
the practice's own pages in `content/seed.json`.

## Article: Teeth Grinding at Night: Signs, Causes and When a Night Guard Helps (`/blog/teeth-grinding-night-guard/`)

- American Dental Association (MouthHealthy), Teeth Grinding: https://www.mouthhealthy.org/all-topics-a-z/teeth-grinding
  (most grinding happens during sleep; signs; causes; "people who smoke or drink alcohol or coffee are twice as likely to grind their teeth"; custom vs store-bought guards; sports guards; stress management; children)
- Cleveland Clinic, Bruxism (Teeth Grinding): https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/10955-teeth-grinding-bruxism
  (awake vs sleep bruxism; symptoms; no single cause; risk factors incl. SSRIs and sleep apnea; complications; muscle relaxants and injections)
- Cleveland Clinic, Mouth Guards: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/10910-mouthguards
  (custom vs store-bought; fit; cleaning; "can last several years")
- NIH National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research, TMD: https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/health-info/tmd
  (conservative, reversible care first; splints should not change the bite)
- Mayo Clinic, Bruxism, diagnosis and treatment: https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/bruxism/diagnosis-treatment/drc-20356100
  (tongue up, teeth apart, lips closed; short-term muscle relaxant at bedtime; injections when other treatments have not helped; damage to restorations and crowns)

## Page: Dental Crowns in McLean, VA (`/services/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns/`)

- Cleveland Clinic, Dental Crowns: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/10923-dental-crowns
  (definition; reasons; materials and their pros and cons; two-visit process; impressions with putty or a digital scanner; temporary crown; two to three weeks at the lab; sensitivity and recovery; average lifespan five to 15 years; care)
- Cleveland Clinic, Prosthodontics: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23904-prosthodontics
  (prosthodontist: three or four additional years of training)
- MedlinePlus (NIH), Dental crowns: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007631.htm
  (numbing at the first visit; call if your bite does not feel right)
- American Association of Endodontists, Root canal explained: https://www.aae.org/patients/root-canal-treatment/what-is-a-root-canal/root-canal-explained/
  (back teeth generally need a crown after a root canal)
