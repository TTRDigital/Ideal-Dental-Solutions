Checked October 26, 2026: researched, then fact-checked line by line by a second reviewer. Drive times on the pages are approximate, off-peak ranges built from the free-flow figures below.

Facts about the practice (address, phone, hours, team, services, accepted insurance plans) come from the site's own pages: the footer, /about-us/, /dr-mehdi-adili/ and the "Accepted Insurance Plans" lists on the McLean service pages. The only office is 8280 Greensboro Drive, Unit 105, McLean, VA 22102, confirmed by the practice (via TTR, October 26, 2026).

Photos are the practice's own (team portraits and the office sign). The "which services people search for" lines on the Tysons and Falls Church pages come from the practice's Google Search Console queries (for example "gum grafting tysons va", 462 impressions, and "falls church va" gum grafting, 403).
